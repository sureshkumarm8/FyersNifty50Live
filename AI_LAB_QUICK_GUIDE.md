## 📊 Available Research Types

### 1. **Pattern Discovery** 🔮
**What it does**: Finds recurring bullish/bearish patterns in historical data

**Shows**:
- Total snapshots analyzed
- Bullish pattern count & success rate
- Bearish pattern count & success rate
- **Decision Block**: When to GO LONG / GO SHORT with entry criteria
- PCR extreme levels analysis

**Use When**: You want to know what historically works

---

### 2. **Correlation Analysis** 📈
**What it does**: Discovers relationships between indicators

**Shows**:
- Sentiment ↔ Price correlation
- PCR ↔ Price correlation  
- Volatility ↔ Sentiment correlation
- **Strategy Block**: High probability setups based on correlations
- Expected moves (30-50 pts in 1-2 hours)

**Use When**: You want data-backed entry rules

---

### 3. **Time Analysis** 🕐
**What it does**: Identifies best/worst trading hours

**Shows**:
- Top 3 best hours (highest movement)
- Top 3 worst hours (lowest movement)
- Average moves per hour
- Volatility per hour
- **Rules Block**: Market open/prime hours/close behaviors

**Use When**: You want to optimize trading schedule

---

### 4. **Volatility Patterns** 🌡️
**What it does**: Studies market volatility regimes

**Shows**:
- Average volatility
- High volatility periods count
- Low volatility periods count
- **Strategy Block**: How to trade each regime
  - High vol: wider stops, smaller size
  - Low vol: tighter stops, mean reversion

**Use When**: You want to adjust risk based on market state

---

### 5. **Reversal Signals** ⚠️
**What it does**: Finds sentiment extremes that preceded reversals

**Shows**:
- Total reversals found
- Reversal rate (%)
- Average sentiment at reversal
- Average PCR at reversal
- **Warning Block**: When to expect reversals
- Trading rules for reversal zones

**Use When**: You want to avoid getting caught in reversals

---

### 6. **Winning Setups** 🎯
**What it does**: Identifies high-probability trade setups

**Shows**:
- Perfect bullish setup (sentiment, PCR, expected move)
- Perfect bearish setup (sentiment, PCR, expected move)
- **Entry Checklists**: Step-by-step criteria
- Historical move averages

**Use When**: You want the best risk-reward setups

---

### 7. **Custom Query** 💭
**What it does**: Answer your specific questions

**Examples**:
- "What happens when PCR > 1.2 and sentiment is negative?"
- "Show me moves when sentiment < 30"

**Shows**:
- Matching data points
- Average price moves
- Custom metrics

**Use When**: You have a specific hypothesis to test

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**Q: Results show "Insufficient data"**
A: Need minimum 50 snapshots. Import more CSV files to Pattern Dashboard.

**Q: Research takes too long**
A: Large datasets (500+ snapshots) may take 5-10 seconds. This is normal.

**Q: Numbers don't make sense**
A: Check archived data quality. Ensure CSV imports were successful.

**Q: Can't see decision blocks**
A: Scroll down - results are hierarchical. Summary → Decisions → Details.

**Q: Export button not working**
A: Check browser download settings. JSON file downloads automatically.
