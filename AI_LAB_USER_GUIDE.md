## 🧠 How It Works

### Multi-Agent System
6 specialized AI agents analyze the market in real-time:

1. **Pattern Recognition** (Blue)
   - Compares current conditions with historical data
   - Finds similar past patterns and their outcomes
   - Confidence based on similarity strength

2. **Sentiment Fusion** (Purple)
   - Aggregates stock sentiment, option sentiment, and overall market mood
   - Weighted combination (50% overall, 30% stocks, 20% options)
   - Detects strong bullish/bearish bias

3. **Momentum Analysis** (Green)
   - Tracks price momentum over last 5 minutes
   - Identifies trending vs ranging markets
   - Measures rate of change

4. **Levels & Timing** (Orange)
   - Analyzes position relative to day high/low
   - Identifies support/resistance zones
   - Best entry points for risk-reward

5. **Risk Calculator** (Red)
   - Monitors volatility levels
   - Warns when risk is elevated
   - Approves favorable conditions

6. **Ensemble Decision** (Indigo)
   - Combines all agent votes
   - Weighted consensus algorithm
   - Final BUY/SELL/HOLD recommendation

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### No Recommendation Showing
- Wait for data to load (30+ snapshots needed)
- Check if agents are analyzing
- Click refresh if stale

### All Agents Say HOLD
- Market is ranging/unclear
- No strong bias detected
- Wait for better setup
- This is GOOD - protects capital!

### Agents Disagree
- Normal! Markets are uncertain
- Lower confidence = skip trade
- Wait for better alignment

### Wrong Predictions
- Markets are probabilistic, not deterministic
- Even 80% confidence means 20% chance of loss
- Focus on win rate over many trades
- Use stop losses to limit damage
